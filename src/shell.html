Deepacket decodes .pcap and .pcapng files in this browser tab, charts the traffic, maps who talked to whom, and reconstructs any intrusion step by step against MITRE ATT&CK.

Or drop a file anywhere on this page. Nothing is uploaded.

Free and open source. Star or watch it on GitHub to hear about new detections and releases.

The sample capture

Traffic

Throughput by protocol over time, top talkers, services and packet sizes. Every chart follows the time window and filter you set.

Threats

Scans, brute force, web exploits, reverse shells, beaconing, DNS tunnelling, exfiltration, ARP spoofing and floods, ordered into an attack flow.

Network map

A network diagram with the internet above your local network, device boxes for every host and attack paths as numbered arrows.

Packets

A virtualized packet list that stays responsive on large captures, with a linked protocol tree and hex view, and full TCP stream reassembly.
